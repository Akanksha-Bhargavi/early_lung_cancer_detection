
### **Contributor Code of Conduct**

#### **Expected Behavior**  
The following behaviors are expected from all team members to create a positive and productive working environment:
- Using respectful, inclusive language in all forms of communication.
- Encouraging and appreciating diverse opinions, experiences, and perspectives.
- Providing thoughtful, constructive feedback and critiques when reviewing teammates' work.
- Offering assistance to fellow team members when needed, ensuring equal collaboration.
- Demonstrating empathy and understanding towards each teammate, fostering a supportive atmosphere.

#### **Unacceptable Behavior**  
Certain behaviors are considered unacceptable and will not be tolerated under any circumstances:
- Use of offensive, inappropriate, or sexualized language or imagery.
- Any form of harassment, whether public or private, towards teammates.
- Engaging in personal, religious, or political attacks.
- Any conduct deemed inappropriate, unwelcome, or unprofessional that disrupts the working environment.

#### **Our Responsibilities**  
All project contributors are responsible for upholding this code of conduct. This includes clearly communicating what constitutes acceptable behavior and taking action to address any inappropriate actions fairly and consistently.

#### **Consequences**  
Project maintainers have the authority to remove or reject contributions that violate the standards set in this code of conduct. This includes, but is not limited to, removing offensive comments, edits, or commits. Maintainers may also take further steps, such as temporarily or permanently banning a contributor for behavior that is harmful, inappropriate, or offensive to the team.

#### **Reporting and Enforcement**  
Instances of unacceptable behavior can be reported through direct communication with any project member via WhatsApp or email. The team will review and investigate any complaints promptly, ensuring confidentiality for the person reporting the issue. If necessary, appropriate actions will be taken to address the situation.

If a project maintainer fails to uphold or enforce the code of conduct in good faith, other team members have the right to initiate discussions on the matter and take further steps to ensure accountability.

#### **Equal Contribution Commitment**  
Each member is committed to contributing equally to the project’s success. In-person meetings will be held after classes (Monday and Friday) on the TRU campus to discuss ongoing tasks, review progress, and address any challenges. By adhering to these values and guidelines, we ensure a collaborative and inclusive environment where everyone’s contribution is valued.
