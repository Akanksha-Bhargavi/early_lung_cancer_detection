### **Team Contract for Project (ADSC 3910)**

#### **Expectations**  
Each team member is expected to contribute approximately 10-12 hours per week toward the ADSC 3910 project. This time includes meetings and independent work on assigned tasks. Any decision to increase the workload beyond this will be made with the agreement of all team members.

All individual tasks must be completed at least 24 hours prior to the submission deadline for any deliverables. This allows adequate time for review, feedback, and necessary revisions before the final submission.

If a team member cannot meet their task deadline or the expected weekly time commitment, they are required to notify the team via WhatsApp or email no less than 48 hours before the deadline. This communication will help the group decide whether to redistribute tasks or extend the timeline, if feasible.

All work submitted by team members should adhere to the instructions and standards outlined for each deliverable to maintain project quality.

#### **Mode of Communication**  
Communication will occur through a variety of channels to ensure efficient collaboration. The team will use:
- **Email** for formal communication and deliverable-related discussions.
- **WhatsApp** for quick and informal communication, updates, and scheduling.
- **GitHub** for submitting and reviewing work, as well as managing the project repository.
- **In-person meetings** will be held on the TRU campus every Monday and Friday after classes. These meetings will focus on task planning, brainstorming, and weekly deliverables review.

In addition, the team will have weekly online check-ins through WhatsApp to provide updates and address any immediate concerns.

#### **Task Management System**  
To organize the tasks effectively, the team will use Google Sheets and Google Docs. Google Sheets will be used to maintain a project task board, where tasks will be assigned, tracked, and marked as completed. Each deliverable will have corresponding tasks, and these will be regularly updated to reflect progress.

Google Docs will serve as a shared space for collaborative writing and developing content. The document will track ongoing contributions to ensure consistency and shared accountability.

#### **Reviews**  
All members are required to create a separate branch in GitHub for their work and submit a pull request for team review before merging it into the main branch. Every pull request should be reviewed by at least one team member within 24 hours of submission to ensure it meets the deliverable criteria and project standards. The review process will provide opportunities for feedback and necessary adjustments before the final submission.

#### **Breach of Contract**  
To maintain accountability, any violation of the team contract will follow a structured process:
1. **First Warning**: A written notification on WhatsApp or email will detail the contract violation, serving as a reminder of the agreed-upon team standards.
2. **Second Warning**: A meeting will be arranged with the team to address the behavior. A formal written agreement will be made with the team member to commit to adhering to the contract going forward.
3. **Final Step**: If issues persist, the team will involve the project mentor to mediate the situation and explore further steps. The mentor’s guidance will determine how to proceed based on the team member’s contributions and conduct.
